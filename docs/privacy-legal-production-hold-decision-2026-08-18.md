# Privacy and Legal production hold

This is a decision packet, not legal approval and not public policy text.

Production processing for consultation attribution, Ask AI, Chatwoot handoff, CRM delivery,
Turnstile, and other providers remains held. Before activation, the authoritative Privacy and Legal
reviewers must approve:

- the purpose and lawful basis for each processing path;
- exact retention and deletion periods by data category;
- consent and suppression evidence handling;
- legal-hold handling;
- each processor and the applicable provider terms;
- cross-border processing and disclosure wording;
- access, correction, complaint, withdrawal, and deletion handling; and
- the exact public notice text and effective date.

Until that signed decision exists, the website must keep affected integrations off, must not state
an exact retention period as authoritative, and must not represent this packet as legal advice or
approval.
